Who Are We? The Story That Captivated a Nation

In September 2019, we made a life-altering decision. What was supposed to be a short family getaway evolved into an epic 6-year journey around the globe. What started as a personal dream rapidly transformed into an unprecedented media and social phenomenon in Israel.

Today, we are the most recognized and influential traveling family in the country. We don't just 'travel'—for hundreds of thousands of people, we symbolize the courage to pursue dreams, embrace radical life changes, and choose the road less traveled.
